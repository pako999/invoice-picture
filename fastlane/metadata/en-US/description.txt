Photograph a paper invoice and send it to your accounting software's email in seconds — with a single click. No manual entry, no lost receipts.

Slikaj Račun works with every Slovenian accounting program that accepts invoices by email with OCR: Minimax, Birokrat, Pantheon, SAOP, E-računi and Metakocka. You set the import email address once, then send every invoice from your phone in seconds.

How it works:
1. Photograph the paper invoice (or upload a PDF).
2. One click — the invoice is sent to your accounting software's email.
3. The software recognises the data via OCR and enters it into your books.

Features:
• One-click sending
• Invoice archive with preview
• Real-time delivery status
• Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP and PDF
• Secure sign-in (Clerk)
• GDPR compliant

Requires an accounting program that supports receiving invoices by email with OCR.
